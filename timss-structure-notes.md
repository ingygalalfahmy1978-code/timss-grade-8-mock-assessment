# TIMSS 2011 Grade 8 Structure Notes

## Standard TIMSS Grade 8 Assessment Structure
- Each TIMSS booklet contains approximately 28-30 items per session
- Two sessions: Mathematics session and Science session
- Each session is 45 minutes
- Total: ~56-60 items per student booklet (split across math and science)
- Multiple booklets exist with different item rotations (matrix sampling)
- Released items from TIMSS 2011 Grade 8 Math PDF: 120 items total (index shows items up to page 120)
- Released items from TIMSS 2011 Grade 8 Science PDF: similar count

## Content Domains (Math)
- Number (Fractions and Decimals, Integers, Ratio/Proportion/Percent, Expressions/Equations)
- Algebra (Patterns, Algebraic Expressions, Equations/Inequalities, Relationships/Functions)
- Geometry (Geometric Shapes, Geometric Measurement, Location/Movement)
- Data and Chance (Data Organization/Representation, Data Interpretation, Chance)

## Content Domains (Science)
- Biology (Characteristics/Life Processes, Cells/Reproduction, Heredity/Evolution, Ecosystems/Human Health)
- Chemistry (Classification/Composition, Properties/Changes)
- Physics (Physical States/Changes, Energy/Heat/Light/Sound, Electricity/Magnetism, Forces/Motion)
- Earth Science (Earth's Structure, Earth's Processes, Earth's History, Earth in Solar System, Climate)

## Cognitive Domains
- Knowing (25%)
- Applying (45%)
- Reasoning (30%)

## Target for Mock Exams
- Each mock: 14-15 Math questions + 14-15 Science questions = ~28-30 questions per session
- Total per mock: ~28-30 questions (one session booklet style)
- OR full booklet: ~56-60 questions (both sessions)
- Current platform uses both sessions, so target: 28-30 math + 28-30 science = ~56-60 per mock
